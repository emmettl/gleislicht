# Ticino route inventory

All 5'142 national GTFS route records and 34'499'152 stop-time rows were scanned. 209 route records from 32 agency identities have a call in the complete canton polygon on at least one archived trip. 28 are inactive on both selected dates. Counts are GTFS identities, not unique public line numbers or legal companies.

Canton membership is independent of operator and selected dates. The machine inventory also records every national noncandidate with its exclusion reason. Each daily candidate must itself call in Ticino. An admitted journey keeps its entire source stop chain, source service date, shifted civil-day times and boarding rules.

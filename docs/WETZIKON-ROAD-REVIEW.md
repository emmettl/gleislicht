## Evidence

The [pinned public sources](../data/wetzikon-road-review-sources.json) contain a complete detailed-road WFS response with **17 axes**, a complete station response with counter **2788**, and its current collector record. The collector still identifies **Wetzikon: Usterstrasse**, with normal lanes toward Uster and Oberwetzikon. The precise station point remains LV95 **2,701,466.18 / 1,242,980.54**.

The station lies within **0.01 m** of detailed axis **2165**, explicitly classified as `Gemeindestrassen` and owned/maintained by `Gemeinde`. That axis has no numbered-road identifier. The nearest ZH 340 axis is **97.00 m** away, agreeing with the original unmatched result. All 17 detailed axes, including planned roads and other municipal streets, remain competitors in the review.

The municipal axis meets ZH 340 at a separate endpoint, LV95 **2,701,327.62 / 1,242,969.36**, shared by detailed main-road features 317 and 487. This establishes a branch relationship. Neither the station point nor the road classification needs to be altered to make the data fit a main-road recording.
